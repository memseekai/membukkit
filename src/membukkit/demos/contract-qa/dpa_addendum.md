# Data Processing Addendum — Amendment No. 1 to the CloudVault–Meridian MSA

Effective Date: 15 June 2024. This Amendment modifies the Master Services Agreement dated 1 March 2024.

## A. Amended Breach Notification

Section 7.2 of the MSA is replaced in its entirety: CloudVault shall notify Meridian of any personal data breach without undue delay and in any event within 24 hours of becoming aware of it, including a description of the nature of the breach, affected data categories, and remediation steps taken.

## B. Subprocessors

CloudVault maintains a list of approved subprocessors at cloudvault.example/subprocessors. New subprocessors require 30 days advance notice, during which Meridian may object on reasonable data-protection grounds. Current approved subprocessors: HetznerCloud GmbH (infrastructure, Germany), Postmark ApS (transactional email, Denmark).

## C. Data Residency Clarification

Notwithstanding Section 7.1 of the MSA, backup snapshots may additionally be replicated to the Dublin region only. No customer data may leave the EU under any circumstances, including for support access.

## D. Audit Rights

Meridian may conduct one audit per calendar year with 21 days notice, at its own cost. Additional audits are permitted following any personal data breach, at CloudVault's cost.
