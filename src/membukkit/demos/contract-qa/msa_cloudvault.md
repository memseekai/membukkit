# Master Services Agreement — CloudVault GmbH and Meridian Logistics AG

Effective Date: 1 March 2024. Term: 24 months, auto-renewing in 12-month increments unless either party gives 90 days written notice before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 The monthly subscription fee is EUR 14,500 for up to 250 named users. Each additional block of 50 users is EUR 2,200 per month.

3.2 Invoices are issued monthly in advance and payable within 30 days. Late payments accrue interest at 4% above the ECB base rate.

3.3 Fees may increase at renewal by no more than the lesser of 5% or the annual EU HICP inflation rate.

## 5. Service Levels

5.1 CloudVault guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime, measured excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced at least 72 hours in advance.

5.2 If uptime falls below 99.9%, Meridian receives a service credit of 5% of the monthly fee per 0.1% shortfall, capped at 30% of the monthly fee.

5.3 Service credits are Meridian's sole and exclusive remedy for availability failures, except in cases of gross negligence.

## 7. Data Protection

7.1 All customer data is stored and processed exclusively in data centers located in the European Union (Frankfurt and Dublin regions).

7.2 CloudVault shall notify Meridian of any personal data breach within 48 hours of becoming aware of it.

7.3 Upon termination, CloudVault shall return or delete all customer data within 60 days, providing written certification of deletion.

## 9. Liability

9.1 Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months preceding the claim.

9.2 The cap in 9.1 does not apply to breaches of Section 7 (Data Protection), for which liability is capped at three times the annual fees.

## 11. Termination

11.1 Either party may terminate for material breach with 30 days written notice if the breach remains uncured.

11.2 Meridian may terminate for convenience with 180 days written notice, subject to an early termination fee equal to 50% of the fees remaining in the current term.
